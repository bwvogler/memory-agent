# Lentil Soup

A weeknight soup that improves overnight, so it is worth making double.

## Ingredients

- 250g red lentils
- 1 onion, 2 carrots, 2 sticks celery
- 1.2l vegetable stock
- Cumin, smoked paprika, a lemon

## Method

Soften the vegetables, add the spices for a minute, then the lentils and
stock. Simmer 25 minutes until the lentils collapse. Finish with lemon.
