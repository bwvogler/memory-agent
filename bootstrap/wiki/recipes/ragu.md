# Ragù

Long and mostly unattended. Start it before lunch to eat it at dinner.

## Ingredients

- 500g beef mince, 150g pancetta
- 1 onion, 1 carrot, 1 stick celery
- 200ml white wine, 400g tinned tomatoes, 250ml milk

## Method

Brown the pancetta, then the mince. Add the soffritto, then the wine and let
it reduce. Tomatoes and milk in, then the lowest possible heat for three
hours. Season at the end, not the start.
